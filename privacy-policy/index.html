6.2 Property Sales and Contractual Relationships

To arrange consultations and viewings, prepare reservation documents, negotiate agreements, administer property purchases and perform contractual obligations.Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b), where processing is necessary for a contract with the individual or requested pre-contractual steps.

6.3 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

To fulfil applicable accounting, taxation, corporate record-keeping, property transaction, anti-money laundering and other legal obligations.Legal basis: Article 6(1)(c), where a relevant legal obligation applies.

6.4 Customer Relationship Management

To organise enquiries, maintain customer relationships, coordinate sales activities and administer legitimate business communications.Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) or Article 6(1)(f), depending on the processing purpose and circumstances.

6.5 Marketing and Promotional Communications

To communicate information about property developments, launches, events and other relevant opportunities where permitted by law.Legal basis: Consent under Article 6(1)(a), where required, or another lawful basis where applicable electronic communications and data protection legislation permits the relevant activity.

6.6 Website Analytics

To understand website usage and improve website performance and user experience.Legal basis: Consent where required for non-essential analytics technologies, and an appropriate GDPR lawful basis for any associated personal data processing.

6.7 Security and Legal Claims

To protect business systems, prevent unauthorised access, investigate incidents and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.Legal basis: Legitimate interests or compliance with legal obligations, as applicable.Where VRI relies on legitimate interests, the Company will assess the necessity of the processing and balance those interests against the rights and freedoms of the individuals concerned.

15. RIGHTS OF DATA SUBJECTS

Subject to the conditions and limitations established by applicable law, individuals have the following rights:

15.1 Right of Access**The right to request confirmation of whether their personal data is processed and to obtain access to that information.

15.2 Right to Rectification**The right to request correction of inaccurate or completion of incomplete personal data

15.3 Right to Erasure**The right to request deletion of personal data where the applicable legal conditions are satisfied.

15.4 Right to Restriction of Processing**The right to request restriction of processing in circumstances provided by law.

15.5 Right to Data Portability**The right to receive qualifying personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and, where applicable, request its transmission to another controller.

15.6 Right to Object**The right to object to certain processing activities, including processing based on legitimate interests, subject to applicable legal conditions.Individuals have the right to object at any time to processing of their personal data for direct marketing purposes.

15.7 Right to Withdraw Consent**Where processing is based on consent, the right to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

15.8 Rights Concerning Automated Decision-Making**The rights provided by Article 22 GDPR in relation to qualifying decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produce legal or similarly significant effects.

15.9 Right to Lodge a Complaint**The right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.
