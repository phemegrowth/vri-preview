GeminaiX: The Why, What and How.

28 Jul 2023 · Samer obeidat

Introduction

In today's fast-paced world, time is our most valuable resource. Professionals across various sectors are constantly grappling with how to accomplish more within the finite 24-hour window. The advent of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has brought forth a solution to this challenge, and leading the charge is GeminaiX, a revolutionary platform that empowers professionals to create their personal digital AI twins, known as Geminais.

The Problem

Data suggests that professionals are not fully seizing the day. With over 875 million professionals on LinkedIn alone, 56% of them work over 70 hours per week, and 60% of their working hours are spent on repetitive, recurring tasks. Furthermore, professionals are often overwhelmed by numerous communication channels, leading to potential customer loss due to poor communication.

Beyond time constraints, professionals are also limited by geographical location, language, culture, hourly payment rates, and fatigue. These factors prevent experts from reaching out to many markets and customers. On the flip side, billions of people lack access to professional services due to these limitations. For instance, half the world lacks access to professional healthcare services, and 70% of Americans and Canadians lack access to professional legal services.

Adding to these challenges is the looming threat of massive technological unemployment. AI is expected to take most professional jobs in the next couple of years. The current Generative AI wave is already automating many jobs including content creators, music producers, marketers, writers, designers, accountants, financial analysts, TV anchors, and others. Other jobs are getting also disrupted including doctors and lawyers. For example, Google recently launched its Med-PaLM2 digital doctor with the well-known Mayo Clinic to offer AI medical services at scale. Med-PaLM 2 has reached 86.5% on the USMLE exam compared to 65% to the average score by doctors. GPT4 also can score around 86% knowing that it is general purpose so you can imagine with GPT 5 is launched.

TV and movie actors are striking to find a solution regarding the digital AI likeness and the concerns of using AI clones without copyrights. According to a New York Times article, the Hollywood actors’ union approved a strike for the first time in 43 years, bringing the $134 billion American movie and television business to a halt over anger about pay and fears of a tech-dominated future. The leaders of SAG-AFTRA, the union representing 160,000 television and movie actors, announced the strike after negotiations with studios over a new contract collapsed, with streaming services and artificial intelligence at the center of the standoff. Actors worry that the technology could be used to create digital replicas of their likenesses (or that performances could be digitally altered) without payment or approval.

Also, over 41% of the code on GitHub is generated by generative AI, not human programmers. This raises concerns about the future of programming jobs and the quality of code being produced.

The Solution: GeminaiX

GeminaiX offers a solution to these challenges by harnessing the power of AI. With an estimated growth of $15.7 trillion by 2030, AI is transforming the way professionals and organizations work. It automates human intelligence by performing repetitive and specific tasks faster and more accurately. AI can read and analyze documents, identify people and things, offer accurate predictions, hyper-personalize professional services at scale, and interact with people 24/7, in multiple languages, simultaneously.

GeminaiX is a decentralized AI platform that allows users to create AI Twins (or Geminais) with the capacity to interact with other humans and Geminais, at scale. These Geminais can hold conversations and perform real-world use cases such as booking appointments, taking meeting notes, and offering professional services in different languages to thousands of people simultaneously.

The Journey to a Fully Functional Geminai

The journey to a fully functional Geminai requires a collaborative effort between the user and GeminaiX technologies. Users actively and passively capture data through manual and automated methods to feed their Geminai. This process ensures that each Geminai is a unique and accurate representation of its human counterpart, capable of providing high-quality, personalized services.

Why Now?

Mega-corporations monopolize AI and utilize people’s digital footprint to increase their profit margin. With the rise of AI, big data, and advanced computer-generated imagery (CGI), these corporations have started developing a new breed of sophisticated, interactive, and self-learning digital AI humans (DAIH) that work for corporations and governments at near-zero marginal cost and outperform white-collar human professionals in their jobs in almost all aspects. It is estimated that AI will replace 800 million blue and white-collar professionals by 2030.

GeminaiX: Power to the People

GeminaiX is about democratizing personal AI ownership and giving the power to the people. It enables professionals to take control of their digital footprint, which mega corporations are already harnessing, to construct personal digital AI twins that mirror and augment them in the digital verse.

The Journey Ahead: Join Us!

GeminaiX is all about collaboration – between you and your digital AI twin. Our pursuit for synergy between the digital and physical worlds stems from our belief in the power of human-to-human collaboration. This is why we started this journey as a team of product masterminds and deep learning experts with decades of experience in building AI solutions.

Going forward, securing funding will enable us to refine the platform’s development, drive the marketing of the product to the professionals and users who need their services, and handle related administrative and legal bottlenecks.

GeminaiX is an evolving process; we are learning as we toe this path. Our faith in this project is rooted in our community of Geminai owners. Together, we can evolve and build a project that reshapes the global landscape and guides humanity towards an AI utopia.

In this future, you own all the cards because GeminaiX is about democratizing personal AI ownership and giving the power to the people. We can recreate the world, one Geminai at a time.
